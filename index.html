des Georges Lab

Department of Molecular Pathobiology
NYU College of Dentistry

With the goal of helping society cure important diseases, the des Georges lab uses cryo-electron microscopy, biophysics, and machine learning to study the structure and dynamics of biological macromolecules important to human physiology.

Our team strives to create a collaborative environment that trains independent scientists who enjoy exchanging ideas and thinking outside the box.
